# Brotherhood of Tarbos edge case: meet and defeat Thornahuun BEFORE finding the Amberstar
# (original game and Advanced, any language). Start: --load 0
# The coffin hall (map 433) is reachable without the Amberstar: the encounter (chain 3 at 3..7,6) is between the
# entrance (9,4) and does not need door 31. Both sub quests are independent:
# - DefeatThornahuun becomes active/completed on its own, BringTheAmberstar stays inactive (hidden) until the
#   Amberstar is found, then it is active until door 31 is opened.
waitidle 20000
dismissall
cheat light 3
cheat godmode 1
# crystal harp from Matthias (NPC 29, other quest)
cheat give 159 1
goto 431 11 24
trigger 11 24 item:159
talkwait
dismissall
goto 431 19 16
wait 2000
dismissall
# --- Thornahuun first
goto 433 5 6
waitbattle 20000
quest BrotherhoodOfTarbos_DefeatThornahuun Active
quest BrotherhoodOfTarbos_BringTheAmberstarToTheTemple Inactive
cheat win
talkwait
takeloot
closewindow
talkwait
goto 433 9 3
wait 2000
dismissall
quest BrotherhoodOfTarbos_DefeatThornahuun Completed
quest BrotherhoodOfTarbos_BringTheAmberstarToTheTemple Inactive
reloadquests
quest BrotherhoodOfTarbos_DefeatThornahuun Completed
quest BrotherhoodOfTarbos_BringTheAmberstarToTheTemple Inactive
# --- now Godsbane and the Amberstar
goto 430 7 8
trigger 7 7 Eye
answer 111
talkwait
dismissall
goto 430 13 4
trigger 13 3 Eye
talkwait
takeloot
count 375 1
closewindow
talkwait
quest BrotherhoodOfTarbos_BringTheAmberstarToTheTemple Active
# --- door 31
goto 433 8 8
trigger 8 9 Eye
talkwait
usekey 375
talkwait
dismissall
quest BrotherhoodOfTarbos_BringTheAmberstarToTheTemple Completed
reloadquests
quest BrotherhoodOfTarbos_BringTheAmberstarToTheTemple Completed
quest BrotherhoodOfTarbos_DefeatThornahuun Completed
exit
